# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ride2fde to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, background, identity,
experience level, nationality, appearance or beliefs. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an
open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

**Things that make the community better:**

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Owning our mistakes, apologising, and learning from them.
- Thinking about what is best for the whole community, not only ourselves.

**Things that are not acceptable:**

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling or discriminatory comments of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an address or phone number, without their permission.
  This matters extra here, because the app is about real people's locations and contact details.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, comments) and when someone is representing the
project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove comments,
commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently
ban anyone for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. They will explain the reason.

**To report a problem,** contact the maintainer, Eshwar Vemula ([@eshwarsgithub](https://github.com/eshwarsgithub)),
using the contact details on <https://www.veshwar.dev/>. Reports are handled privately and respectfully.
