Why this exists

Meet before the summit. Your first session starts on the road.

Kandi is a long way from most of Hyderabad. Share the ride and the commute becomes time to meet the people you will spend the day with.

Network on the way
Arrive having already met people who build what you build.
Split the cost
Share the fare or the fuel instead of everyone paying alone.
Wait less
One shared ride means fewer cab bookings and less waiting around.

How it works

  1. 01

    Pick your side

    Choose Ride giver if you have a car or bike with room for someone (and say which), or Ride taker if you need a ride.

  2. 02

    Pin your start

    Type your area or use your location. We draw your driving route to IIT Hyderabad and show the distance and time.

  3. 03

    See who is on your way

    Anyone starting within about 2 km of another person's route shows up, in pickup order for drivers.

  4. 04

    Say hi your way

    Pick LinkedIn, email, WhatsApp, or a call or text, whichever that person shared, and agree the pickup point and time.

Ride giver

You have room to share

Your route is drawn in lime. Riders along it are listed in the order you would pick them up, with how far off your route each one is.

Ride taker

You need a seat

Drivers whose route passes near you are shown first, with their route in cyan. Others in your area are grouped by zone.

Good to know

  • You choose what to share: LinkedIn, email, WhatsApp, a phone number, or nothing. Contact options show only on people whose route matches yours.
  • Your start is shown as a rough area, not your address.
  • There is no login. Anyone with this link can see names, rough areas, routes and the contact details people added, so keep the link inside attendee groups.
  • The distance and time are a single driving estimate made when you add your route, not live traffic.
